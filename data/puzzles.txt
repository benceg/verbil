# STARTER [adjective|noun]          (a verb unless marked)
#   definition of the one sense the puzzle is about: WORD WORD WORD ...
#     (answers may continue on further indented lines)
#
# Answers are synonyms of that sense, and for verbs specific kinds of the action too
# (RUN takes TROT and GALLOP). The definition is shown under the starter, so it must not
# contain any answer (or the starter) in any form; build.py checks. A/B means B is
# accepted as an alternative spelling of A. 16-45 answers per puzzle, 20+ wherever the
# sense allows. A starter may return later in the year with a different sense.
# Curated by hand from WordNet, cross-checked against a thesaurus. Day one (RUN) is
# fixed; the rest are shuffled so that similar puzzles are spread apart. Run
# scripts/build.py after editing, and never change or reorder days already played.
